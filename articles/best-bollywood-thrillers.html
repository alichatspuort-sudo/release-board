5 Bollywood Thrillers Worth Watching (Spoiler-Free)

Hindi cinema has produced some sharp thrillers in the last decade. Here are five to start with. None of the notes below give away the ending.

Kahaani (2012)

Directed by Sujoy Ghosh. A pregnant woman searches for her missing husband in Kolkata. Tight pacing and a memorable setting.

Drishyam (2015)

Directed by Nishikant Kamat. A family man is pulled into a police investigation. A slow build that rewards patience.

Talvar (2015)

Directed by Meghna Gulzar. A crime drama that shows the same case from different points of view.

Andhadhun (2018)

Directed by Sriram Raghavan. A dark comedy thriller about a pianist and a chain of bad decisions.

Badla (2019)

Directed by Sujoy Ghosh. A lawyer and a businesswoman talk through a night that changed both their lives.

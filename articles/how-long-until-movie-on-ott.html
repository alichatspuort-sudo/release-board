How Long Until a Theatrical Movie Reaches OTT?

There is no single rule. The gap between a cinema release and a streaming release depends on the studio, the country and how the film performed.

The usual pattern

Films first run in cinemas. Later they often become available to rent or buy digitally. Streaming on a subscription service usually comes after that. In recent years many studios have shortened these gaps, and some deals now bring films to streaming within weeks or a couple of months.

Why it varies

Studios and platforms sign deals that set the timing. A big hit may stay in cinemas longer. A film that did poorly may move to streaming sooner.

Bollywood and Hollywood

Both industries have used a window of several weeks between cinema and streaming, but the exact number differs by film and changes over time.

How to find out

Announcements usually appear a few weeks before the date. Use the Release Board and check the Just released list and each movie's Where to watch section.

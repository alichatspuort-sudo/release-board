How to Check Where a Movie Is Streaming in Pakistan

Streaming rights are sold country by country. A film on Netflix in the US may be on a different platform in Pakistan, or not streaming at all. That is why a friend's tip from another country often turns out wrong.

The fast way

Open the Release Board, set the country to Pakistan, and search for the title. Tap the movie and look at the Where to watch section. Platforms marked as streaming are included with a subscription. Rent and buy options cost extra per film.

If the movie is not listed

That usually means it is not on any platform in your country yet. New releases often stay in cinemas first, then go to rent or buy, and only later to a subscription service.

Before you subscribe

Availability changes without notice, so check the platform's own page before paying. Some titles leave a service at the end of a licensing deal, sometimes within weeks of arriving.

Tip

If you only want one movie, check whether renting it costs less than a month of subscription.

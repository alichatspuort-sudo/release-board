How to Never Miss a Movie Release: 4 Easy Ways

New movies come out every week and it is easy to miss the ones you would have liked. These four habits help.

1. Use a release calendar

The Release Board lists upcoming films by date. Check it once a week, and switch between Hollywood and Bollywood.

2. Watch trailers early

Trailers usually appear weeks before release. Watching them tells you quickly whether a film is for you.

3. Keep a short watchlist

Write down three or four titles you want to see. A short list is easier to follow than a long one.

4. Check your country

Release dates and streaming platforms differ by country. Set your country before you plan a movie night.

Do this once a week and you will rarely miss a release.

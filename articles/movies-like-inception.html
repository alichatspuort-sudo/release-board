6 Movies Like Inception for Mind-Bending Fans

Inception (2010) mixes a heist story with dream logic. If that combination worked for you, these films go in a similar direction.

Interstellar (2014)

Also directed by Christopher Nolan. Space travel, time and family, with a big emotional core.

The Prestige (2006)

Nolan again. Two rival magicians and a rivalry that goes too far.

Memento (2000)

A man with short-term memory loss tries to solve a crime. The story is told in an unusual order.

Shutter Island (2010)

Directed by Martin Scorsese. A US Marshal investigates a disappearance at a psychiatric hospital.

Arrival (2016)

Directed by Denis Villeneuve. A linguist tries to communicate with visitors. A thoughtful science fiction film.

Tenet (2020)

Nolan's film about moving through time in unusual ways. Best watched with full attention.

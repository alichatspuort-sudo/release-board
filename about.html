About

Release Board helps you find out when Hollywood and Bollywood movies release and which streaming platform to watch them on in your country.

What you will find here

  • Release dates for upcoming and recent films
  • Trailers, ratings, cast and short synopses
  • Where each film is streaming, for rent, or for sale in Pakistan, India, the US, the UK and the UAE

Where the data comes from

Movie information and images are provided by TMDB. Streaming availability is provided by JustWatch through TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

A note on accuracy

Release dates and platform availability change often. Check the platform before you subscribe. We do not host, stream or link to pirated movies.
